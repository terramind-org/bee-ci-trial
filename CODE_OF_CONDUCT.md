# Code of Conduct

Treat other participants with respect, discuss technical disagreements directly, protect private information, and keep contributions reviewable. This applies in every project space, including issues, reviews, chats, documentation, and contributions made by agents and by the people who operate them.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and using an agent to evade these standards are unacceptable.

Report a conduct concern through the maintainers' private contact listed in this repository's Security tab. Do not post a report or personal information in a public project space. Maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as they can, may request context, and may respond with guidance, a warning, removal of content, or removal of participation access. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
